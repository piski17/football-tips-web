How it works

Most tips are a guess. Ours need a verdict.

A single opinion can be wrong. So every TIPREX tip has to convince two independent judges, and the price has to be right, before it reaches you.

1 Judge one

The statistical model

Goals, corners, cards, shots and form of both teams, home and away, head-to-head and missing players. It turns the numbers into a probability and needs to see at least +5 % value.

2 Judge two

The sharpest market

We strip the margin from the odds of the world's sharpest bookmaker, where professionals bet. It has to agree that the price is good too. If the two judges disagree too much, there is no tip.

3 Verdict

The best price

Only odds from 1.70 to 3.20, the best price across major bookmakers, and the exact same bet must be offered by at least three of them, so you can actually place it.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is profit guaranteed?

No, and anyone who promises that is lying. Football is uncertain and losing weeks will happen. Our goal is a long-term edge over the bookmakers, measured over hundreds of tips, and we show every result so you can judge for yourself.

How many tips will I get?

It depends on the fixtures. Some days there are several, on quiet days none at all. A tip only goes out when both judges agree, we never force one to fill a quota.

Which bookmakers do I need?

Any of the major international ones. Every tip must be available at three or more of them, for example Bet365, Unibet, Betano, William Hill or Betway, so you can place it wherever you have an account.

Why are the odds at least 1.70?

Very low odds need a very high hit rate just to break even. From 1.70 up, a solid edge turns into real profit without risking big on a single bet.

What is the "sharp market"?

Some bookmakers take bets from professionals and adjust their prices fast, so their odds are the most accurate estimate of the true chances. We remove their margin and use it as our second, independent judge.

How much should I stake?

We recommend a flat 1–2 % of your betting bankroll per tip. Only bet money you can afford to lose.
